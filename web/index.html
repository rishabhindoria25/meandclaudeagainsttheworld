Each bar is one minute. Filled bars are minutes the compressor was drawing power.

How this is worked out

There is no current sensor in this build, so energy is derived from the unit's nameplate: watts equal BTU ÷ EER, accumulated over the minutes the compressor is actually running. Fan-only minutes are excluded because they draw a rounding error by comparison.

Set your own figures under Setup — they are printed on the label inside the front grille.
